04 — Get help

You don't have to handle it alone.

If you or someone you know has experienced sexual harassment or violence, support is available.

Emergency

If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services.

110

SAPA

The Ministry of Women's Empowerment and Child Protection support service.

129

Campus support

Contact your university's Satgas PPKS or other designated support service.

Legal assistance

Consider contacting a trusted legal aid organisation if you need legal guidance.

1

Make sure you're safe

Move somewhere safe and contact someone you trust if necessary.

2

Preserve evidence

Keep relevant screenshots, messages, documents or other evidence.

3

Find support

Speak to someone you trust or contact an appropriate support organisation.
